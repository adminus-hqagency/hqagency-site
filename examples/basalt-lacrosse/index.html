Questions parents ask

My child has never played. Is that okay?

Absolutely. U10 and U12 programs are built for new players, and our gear library covers equipment for the first season.

How much time does a season take?

Most teams practice twice a week with one game or tournament on weekends. Travel teams add a few out-of-town weekends.

Is financial help available?

Yes. Need-based scholarships are available for every program. Just check the box when you register.
