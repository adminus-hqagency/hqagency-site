Services

What we do

Two services: a new website built from scratch, or a refresh of the one you have. Every project is quoted after a short conversation.

How it works

From first message to live site

  1. Brief

    You tell us about your business, your customers and what the site needs to do.

  2. Look

    We design a look from your own name, logo and trade, so it doesn't look like anyone else's.

  3. Build

    We build the pages, and you review them on your own phone and laptop before anything goes live.

  4. Launch

    We put the site live, connect your contact form and check every page.

Before we start

What helps us most

Your logo and colors

Any file format is fine. If you don't have a logo yet, tell us.

Real photos

Your shop, your team, your work. Phone photos of the real thing beat stock images.

Sites you like

A few links to sites you like, or don't, help us understand your taste quickly.
