Services

New sites and refreshes.

Two services, done properly. Here's what each one includes and who it's for.

Service 1

A new website

For businesses with no website yet, or a site so far gone it's easier to start again.

Good fit for

  • New businesses
  • Businesses running on a social page alone
  • Rebrands
  • Sites built on a dead template

We start with a short brief: what you do, who your customers are, and what you want them to do when they land on your site. From there we design a look built from your own name, logo and trade, then build the pages.

  • A custom design built from your brand
  • Core pages: home, services, about and contact (more if you need them)
  • Built for phones first, then tablets and desktops
  • A working contact form, plus tap-to-call and tap-to-email links
  • Page titles, descriptions and sharing previews set up for Google and social
  • Accessibility basics: readable contrast, labelled forms, keyboard navigation

Service 2

A website refresh

For businesses whose site still works, but looks dated, loads slowly or is hard to use on a phone.

Good fit for

  • Sites that look dated
  • Slow or clunky pages
  • Sites that break on phones
  • Hard-to-find contact info

A refresh keeps what's already working for you: your web address, your content and the page addresses people and search engines already know. We rebuild the design and the parts that slow people down.

  • A review of your current site: what to keep, fix and drop
  • A new, modern design that still feels like your business
  • Faster pages and properly sized images
  • A clearer path to calling, booking or getting in touch
  • Existing page addresses kept or redirected, so search results still work
  • Your current booking, payment or ordering tools linked in, not replaced
Ask about a refresh

How it works

Four steps, start to finish.

  1. Brief

    We ask about your business, your customers and what the site needs to do for you.

  2. Look

    We design a look from your own name, logo and trade, so it doesn't look like anyone else's.

  3. Build

    We build the pages and you review them on your own phone and laptop before anything goes live.

  4. Launch

    We put the site live, connect your contact form and check every page before handing it over.

Before we start

What helps us most.

You don't need all of this on day one. We'll ask for what's missing as we go.

Your logo

Any format is fine. If you don't have one, tell us.

Real photos

Your shop, your team, your work. Phone photos of the real thing beat stock images.

Your details

Services, hours, service area and how customers should reach you.

Sites you like

A few links to sites you like (or dislike) help us understand your taste quickly.
